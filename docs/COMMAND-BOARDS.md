# Command boards — v0.2.0 review draft

Existing source Order effects remain defined by the document and player rulings. Individual success costs, new faction identities and every new ongoing trait below are PROVISIONAL for Nathan’s review. They are playable proposals, not additional canonical source material.

One quality die per deployed troop or commander; 4+ successes are assigned directly. Natural maximums explode recursively, failures are spent. No automatic initiative replacement. An empty bowl can be rolled again; any armed or held dice block refill. At the start of each own initiative, one die may be discarded, including an assigned die. Discarding one die from an armed basket returns the other dice to the bowl; releasing a slot spends its assigned dice. An ongoing effect ends as soon as its die is removed.

## Board selection and ongoing traits

Choose one board per army and optionally one matching subfaction during setup. Every identity effect must be activated on its legal selected target; no free passives. Each trait costs one success and lasts while held.

### The Athenian Order

Disciplined combined action, controlled fire lanes and command intelligence.

- **Cohesion Protocol — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. That unit gains one Advantage on Rally actions while this success die remains committed.
- **Calculated Execution — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. That unit gains one Advantage on Skill actions while this success die remains committed.
- **Citadel Discipline — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. While that unit is in terrain, incoming shooting against it suffers one additional Disadvantage while this success die remains committed.

### Expeditionary Accord — PROVISIONAL

Independent frontier forces, practical skills, recovery and mobile support.

- **Field Autonomy — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. That unit gains one Advantage on Skill actions while this success die remains committed.
- **Surveyed Fire Lane — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate against one enemy Infantry unit in terrain. Reduce that target’s terrain-cover Disadvantage against your incoming shooting by one while this success die remains committed. Never reduce cover below zero.
- **Recovery Drill — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. That unit gains one Advantage on Rally actions while this success die remains committed.

### Obsidian Dominion — PROVISIONAL

Deliberate pressure, close assault, armored firepower and refusal to yield.

- **Conquest Imperative — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. That unit gains one Advantage on Melee actions while this success die remains committed.
- **Bastion Link — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Vehicle unit. While that vehicle is in terrain, incoming shooting against it suffers one additional Disadvantage while this success die remains committed.
- **Predator Focus — PROVISIONAL, 1 held die:** Activate on one friendly Infantry unit. That unit gains one Advantage on Shooting actions while this success die remains committed.
